**Participant task description**

In this study, you will take part in a collective decision about how to allocate a charitable donation. You will be asked to decide **which charity should receive a $10 donation**.

Several charities are working on different approaches to addressing social problems. Each of them uses donations in different ways and focuses on different types of impact.

In an anonymous conversation with a neutral AI assistant, you will be asked to **rank the options and** **briefly explain why exactly you prefer your top choice**. After everyone in the group makes their choice, the charity that receives the most support will be selected as the winner.

Before making your decision, please read the descriptions of the charities carefully. As you read them, try to think about:

* what effects the donation could have  
* which problems you think are most important to address  
* why you think one option may be better than the others

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in how people think about these choices.

**Charity options**

## **1\. Community Animal Rescue Shelter**

[*Animal Haven*](https://animalhaven.org/) 

A nonprofit animal rescue organization that takes in abandoned or neglected dogs and cats in New York City. The shelter provides food, medical care, behavioral support, and temporary housing while working to find permanent adoptive homes. Donations help cover veterinary treatment, shelter operations, and adoption programs that give homeless animals a chance to live in safe, long-term homes.

---

## **2\. Community Food Pantry Network**

[*City Harvest*](https://www.cityharvest.org/)

A nonprofit organization that helps address food insecurity in New York City by collecting surplus food from restaurants, farms, and grocery stores and delivering it to food pantries, soup kitchens, and community programs. Donations support food distribution, transportation, and partnerships with local organizations that provide meals and groceries to families experiencing hunger.

---

## **3\. Urban Tree Initiative**

[*Trees New York*](https://treesny.org/)

A nonprofit that works with communities across New York City to plant and care for street trees and other urban green spaces. The organization trains volunteers and local residents to maintain trees, organizes planting projects, and promotes stewardship of the city’s urban forest. Donations help fund tree planting, maintenance programs, and environmental education for residents and youth.

---

## **4\. Community Health Outreach**

[*East Harlem Health Outreach Partnership*](https://icahn.mssm.edu/education/medical/clinical/ehhop)

A local nonprofit program that provides basic health services—such as health screenings, vaccinations, and referrals to medical care for people who face barriers to accessing healthcare. They provide care to uninsured adults in their neighborhood, and their services are confidential and provided at no cost.
